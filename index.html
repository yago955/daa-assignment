The six algorithms

Each feature of the engine is one textbook problem.

1KMP string matching

O(n + m)

Finds amenity keywords (AC, wifi, CCTV) in free-text listings, with word-boundary and "non" negation checks.

2Dijkstra (binary heap)

O((V + E) log V)

Shortest commute and actual route from your college to every locality.

3Floyd–Warshall

O(V³)

All-pairs commute table; its rows cross-check Dijkstra exactly.

40/1 Knapsack (DP)

O(n · W)

Best set of add-ons (gym, laundry, dinner…) within a monthly budget.

5Heap-based top-k

O(N log k)

Ranks PGs by rating, rent and commute and keeps only the best k.

6Edmonds–Karp max flow

O(V · E²)

Allots students to PGs under bed limits, budgets and commute limits — provably optimal.
